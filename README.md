# ABOUT MYSELF

**LISTOWELL DEI ANANE**

My name is **Listowell Dei Anane**, and I am a 25-year-old Ghanaian from Dormaa Ahenkro in the Bono Region of Ghana. I was born on 17th November 2000. I am a soldier in the Ghana Armed Forces, a graphic designer, a web designer, a creative entrepreneur, and someone who is passionate about technology, personal development, and building a successful future.

I consider myself a disciplined, respectful, honest, hardworking, and determined young man. I believe in teamwork, continuous learning, and making good use of every opportunity that comes my way. I am someone who believes that success does not happen overnight. It requires patience, consistency, sacrifice, and the willingness to improve every day.

I proudly serve as a soldier in the Ghana Armed Forces. Being in the military has helped me develop discipline, courage, responsibility, teamwork, and leadership skills.

My military career is an important part of my life because it has taught me the value of hard work, time management, respect for authority, and commitment to duty.

Beyond my normal military responsibilities, I have also contributed my creative and technological skills to military-related projects, including graphic design, publications, documentation, and other digital assignments.

Graphic design is more than just a skill to me. It is something I genuinely enjoy because it allows me to transform ideas into creative visual solutions.

I specialize in:

- Logo design and brand identity
- Flyers, posters, and promotional materials
- Social media graphics
- Product packaging and label design
- Business branding and advertising
- Clothing and merchandise designs

I have worked on projects for churches, businesses, entrepreneurs, organizations, and the Ghana Armed Forces, including design work connected with the Army Recruit Training School.

My creative philosophy is that a good design should not only look beautiful but should also communicate effectively and solve a problem.
